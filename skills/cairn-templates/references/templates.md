# Templates

- `shop` — **E-commerce** (mobile): A storefront with cart and checkout
- `dashboard` — **Dashboard** (web): KPIs, charts and an orders table
- `blog` — **Blog** (web): Posts, search, authors and a newsletter
- `docs` — **Documentation** (web): Sidebar, search palette and content blocks
- `landing` — **SaaS landing** (web): Hero, pricing, testimonials and a waitlist
- `jaspr_store` — **Online store (SEO)** (web): Server-rendered, with full SEO
- `onboarding` — **Onboarding** (mobile): Swipeable intro, permissions, personalise
- `auth` — **Authentication** (mobile): Sign in, sign up and recovery
- `chat` — **Chat** (mobile): Conversations, threads and groups
- `app_landing` — **App landing** (web): A page that sells a phone app
- `settings` — **Settings** (mobile): Searchable settings with profile, privacy and more
