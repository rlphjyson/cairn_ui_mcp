# Templates

- `shop` — **E-commerce** (mobile): A storefront with cart and checkout
- `dashboard` — **Dashboard** (web): KPIs, charts and an orders table
- `blog` — **Blog** (web): Posts, search, authors and a newsletter
- `docs` — **Documentation** (web): Sidebar, search palette and content blocks
- `landing` — **SaaS landing** (web): Hero, pricing, testimonials and a waitlist
